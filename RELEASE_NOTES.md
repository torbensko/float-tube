## FloatTube 1.0.1

- New floating-player icon, shared across the app and website.
- Easier installation: open the DMG and drag FloatTube into Applications.
- Universal Apple Silicon and Intel builds, with mandatory Developer ID signing and Apple notarization.
- Stapled app and disk image, ZIP alternative, and SHA256 checksums.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Quit FloatTube before replacing an existing installation. Browser data and window settings are preserved.
